STILLFORM is an original portfolio project by Beibars. An experiment in sound, form, and taking a moment.

The sculpture is drawn live. The sound is synthesized in your browser. There are no accounts, trackers, paid tools, or audio downloads.

Your saved mixes stay on your device. This is a creative listening tool, with no health or productivity promises.
